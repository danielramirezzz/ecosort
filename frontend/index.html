    Which bin does it go in?

    In Colombia, waste is sorted into three colored bins.

    Press and drag a bin to spin it

    Organic · green bin

    Food scraps, fruit and vegetable peels, garden waste.

    Recyclable · white bin

    Clean and dry plastic, glass, metal, paper and cardboard.

    Non-recyclable · black bin

    Used toilet paper and napkins, face masks, cigarette butts and worn-out clothes. Batteries, phones and electronics go to a take-back collection point.

    How it works

    1. 01

      You upload a photo

      The image is sent from this page to the server (FastAPI).

    2. 02

      Preprocessing

      It is resized to 224×224 pixels and normalized with the ImageNet values.

    3. 03

      Neural network

      EfficientNet-B0 with transfer learning computes the probability of organic, recyclable and non-recyclable.

    4. 04

      Confidence threshold

      If the confidence is below —, the model is unsure and the item is treated as non-recyclable to be safe.
